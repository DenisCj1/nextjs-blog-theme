How to Use This Tracker

🐾

Caring for an older dog means watching for small changes before they become big problems. This tracker gives you one simple place to record your dog's daily wellbeing, weight, medications, meals, symptoms, and vet visits — so nothing slips through the cracks, and so your vet gets a clear picture at every appointment.

What's inside

  • Dog Profile & Emergency Contacts — key info in one place.
  • Medication & Supplement Schedule — never miss or double a dose.
  • Weekly & Daily Health Logs — appetite, energy, mobility, mood.
  • Weight & Body Condition Tracker — spot trends early.
  • Vet Visit Records — questions, diagnoses, follow-ups.
  • Symptom Diary — track anything unusual over time.
  • Feeding & Diet Log — food, portions, appetite notes.

Getting started

  • Print the pages you need — reprint the logs as often as you like.
  • Keep it somewhere visible: by the food bowl or the front door.
  • Bring it to every vet visit — the trends you record are gold.
  • Tip: take a photo of completed pages as a backup.

Emergency warning signs — call the vet now

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Collapse or fainting
  • Repeated vomiting / diarrhoea
  • Bloated, hard abdomen
  • Seizures
  • Won't eat or drink 24h+
  • Pale or blue gums
  • Sudden inability to stand
